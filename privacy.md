# Privacy Policy

*Last updated 26 November 2026*

Sepulchre is a Discord bot run by the Sepulchre team. This page explains what Sepulchre stores, why, for how long, and how to have it removed. Sepulchre only works in Discord servers that have added it, and most features are off until that server's staff turn them on.

## What Sepulchre reads

Sepulchre receives what Discord sends a bot in the servers it is in: messages, member joins and leaves, role and channel changes, reactions and voice activity. It reads message text to run commands that start with a prefix (like `,help`) and for features a server turns on, such as filters, auto-responses, highlights, counting and word statistics. Most of what it reads is used right away and not saved.

## What Sepulchre stores

- **Discord IDs and names.** Server, channel, role, message and user IDs, and usernames or display names in moderation cases and settings change records, so staff can see who did what.
- **Server settings.** Whatever staff set up: channels, roles, welcome and goodbye messages, embeds and images they upload, auto-responses, filters, word lists, tags and similar.
- **Activity statistics, when a server turns them on.** Message counts (daily counts are kept 31 days), experience levels, voice levels, streaks, reaction counts, word counts (how often a word was used, not the messages), invite tracking (who invited whom, and join and leave times), command use counts, game scores, giveaway entries and similar.
- **Things you give Sepulchre.** Your AFK status, reminders, tags, highlight words, suggestions, a personal prefix and similar, until you or the server's staff remove them.
- **Moderation and safety records.** Moderation cases with their reasons, warnings and timed actions; the roles of members who leave (kept 30 days, so staff can restore them); raid detection join records (24 hours); anti-nuke action records (7 days); honeypot catches.
- **Ticket transcripts.** When staff export or close a ticket, the messages in it are saved as a web page. It is posted in the server's transcript channel and kept in private storage at Cloudflare, opened only through links that expire after 7 days.
- **Licence records.** Which servers have a Sepulchre licence and who owns it.
- **Short-lived records.** Message IDs used to avoid counting the same message twice (2 to 3 days).

Not stored: deleted messages shown by snipe stay in memory only (at most 10 per channel, gone on restart, and snipe is off unless a server turns it on). Pictures and videos sent to `/gif` are deleted as soon as the GIF is made. Server event logs and the command log are posted in channels the server chooses; Sepulchre does not keep copies.

## Why

Only to run the features a server uses: moderation, statistics, tickets, logs and the rest. Sepulchre does not sell data, show ads, or use it for anything else.

## Who else sees it

Discord, because Sepulchre runs on Discord. Cloudflare, which stores ticket transcripts and hosts these pages. The company hosting Sepulchre's servers. Nobody else, unless the law requires it.

## How long it is kept

Where a time is listed above, that is the limit. Everything else is kept until server staff remove it with Sepulchre's commands, or until you ask for it to be deleted.

## Your rights

You can ask what Sepulchre stores about you, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. A server owner can ask for all of a server's data to be deleted. Contact: our support server, [discord.gg/88nA6GTD7Q](https://discord.gg/88nA6GTD7Q). Requests are answered within 30 days. If you are in the EU or UK, you also have the rights the GDPR gives you, including complaining to your data protection authority.

## Age

Sepulchre is for people old enough to use Discord under Discord's own rules.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Important changes are announced in the support server.
