✧ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions, answered.

Everything you may want to know before joining or purchasing.

What does a license cost?＋

The stated price is $5.99 USD as a one-time purchase for one Discord server. Review our Terms and Refund Policy before buying.

Is there a recurring subscription?＋

Not under the current stated licensing model. Each license is associated with one server.

Where can I purchase a license?＋

Join the official Sepulchre Discord. The website does not take payments or store payment details.

Which payment methods are supported?＋

Crypto, Skrill, Payoneer and Wise are currently listed as supported. Confirm the available method and payment instructions with official staff before sending money.

How do I contact support?＋

Use the official Discord support server linked throughout this website.

Can a license move between servers?＋

The published policies describe one eligible server transfer per rolling 30-day period, subject to their conditions. Check the Terms and Refund Policy for details.

Where can I find all bot commands?＋

The Commands page describes the major areas. Check within Discord for currently available slash commands.
